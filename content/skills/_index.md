These are the agent skills I use every day for pull-request work, CI triage and orchestration. They are published for free on the [Tessl registry](https://tessl.io/registry/mdelapenya/coding-skills) and on [skills.sh](https://skills.sh/mdelapenya/coding-skills). Each page says what the skill does, when to reach for it and what the evals measured. If something does not work for you, or you have a scenario the evals should cover, open an issue or send one at [github.com/mdelapenya/coding-skills](https://github.com/mdelapenya/coding-skills). If the skills are useful to you, you can also [star the repository](https://github.com/mdelapenya/coding-skills), and you can [subscribe to the blog](/subscribe/) to hear when I publish more about them.
