Here is a short case for using an open source product, Liferay Portal, to manage portals.

In a nutshell, Liferay Portal is a portal manager that makes the development team's job much easier when it comes to setting up a portal, because it is so easy to use.

It lets you **manage portals**: a single Liferay can hold as many portals as you need.

Broadly, these are Liferay's main features:

*   Runs on the major application servers and servlet containers, databases and operating systems
*   JSR-286 compliant
*   About 60 portlets preinstalled "out-of-the-box"
*   Content Management System (CMS) and collaboration suite
*   Personalized pages for every user

To dig deeper into Liferay Portal, I recommend visiting the product's official website, [www.liferay.com](http://www.liferay.com/).

Put together, this means you can have a fully functional intranet in very little time, with just a simple initial configuration.

As for the functional reasons to use Liferay as a portal manager, there are 4 main pillars that would make me choose it.

## User management

Most enterprise applications have a repository where they store the users with access to them. It could live in an LDAP or in the database of some specific DBMS.

Liferay can connect to that repository and use it to authenticate users.

## Content management

In the same way, the information needs of companies grow at a fast pace, both in how much they consume and in how much they produce.

Liferay provides a content management system that is easy for administrators to manage and intuitive for end users.

## Document management

Alongside that growing need for information, companies tie it to physical documents. That is why Liferay adds a document manager based on Apache's Jackrabbit project.

## Enterprise software

Finally, companies keep developing enterprise applications in different environments and platforms.

With Liferay, we could design those applications following the Java Portlet specification, so they can be added to the portal and placed wherever we need them.

For these reasons, among others, I think a portal manager like Liferay Portal is the right thing to build on.
