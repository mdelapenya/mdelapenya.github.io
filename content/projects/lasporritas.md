## las porritas

The pool that settles who runs the group. Predict every matchday and the final table across Europe's top five leagues — La Liga, the Premier League, the Bundesliga, Serie A and Ligue 1 — or go all-in on the Champions League. Points for every hit, a live ranking, and no shortage of reasons to keep arguing about football.

Scoring is straightforward: 20 points for a podium call (champion, Champions League spots, relegation), 1 point for a correct match result, and −1 if you leave a match unpredicted. Predictions lock one hour before kick-off — enforced at the database level, not just the UI. Create or join as many pools as you like, each with its own competition and season. Available in English, Spanish, German, Italian and French.

- Live app: [lasporritas.vercel.app](https://lasporritas.vercel.app)
